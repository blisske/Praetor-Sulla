# Foundation Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-05-22
**Operator:** Foundation Bots
**Contact:** support@foundationbots.com

This policy describes what data Foundation collects, why, and what
the operator does with it. Short and plain.

---

## 1. What Foundation Collects

**Account data** (provided directly by you):
- Email address
- Password (stored as a bcrypt-or-argon2 hash; the operator never
  sees plaintext)
- Optional recovery email

**Brokerage credentials** (provided directly by you):
- Kraken API key + secret (encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; see
  Terms of Service §4 for the operator-access disclosure)

**Trading + activity data** (generated by your use of the Service):
- Trade history (orders placed, fills, P&L, simulated trades in
  shadow mode)
- Engine + risk-state events (drawdown halts, mode flips, freeze
  events, broker key validations)
- Audit log of broker-key-touching actions (paste, rotate, disconnect,
  scope changes)
- Login attempts (success/fail/IP/timestamp)

**Technical data** (auto-collected on each request):
- IP address (truncated to /24 in some audit log fields after 30 days)
- Browser user-agent string
- Timestamps + endpoint URLs hit

**What Foundation does NOT collect:**
- Your bank-account details
- Your government-issued ID
- Marketing-tracking cookies
- Third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no
  Segment, etc.)
- Cross-site advertising data

---

## 2. Why Foundation Collects It

| Data type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email + password hash | Account authentication, recovery, email-verification + reset flows |
| Brokerage credentials | Executing trades on your behalf per your configured strategy |
| Trading + activity data | Showing you your dashboard, audit history, P&L, position state |
| Login attempts | Detecting + responding to brute-force attempts |
| IP + user-agent | Anti-abuse, debugging, security incident investigation |

---

## 3. Who Foundation Shares Data With

**Postmark** (transactional email vendor): Your email address +
the contents of system emails sent to you (verification, password
reset, live-mode confirmation codes, etc.). Postmark stores send
history per their own retention policy.

**Cloudflare** (DNS + Email Routing for inbound `@foundationbots.com`
addresses): Inbound email metadata only. Your account password and
brokerage keys never traverse Cloudflare.

**Kraken** (and any other supported brokerage): When Foundation
places, modifies, or cancels orders on your behalf, those requests
hit Kraken's API with the credentials YOU provided. Kraken receives
the requests + your IP-as-seen-from-Foundation's-server (which is
the operator's host IP).

**Law enforcement or legal process:** If served with a valid subpoena,
court order, or other binding legal demand, Foundation will comply
to the extent required by law. The operator will attempt to notify
the affected user(s) unless legally prohibited from doing so.

**That's the whole list.** Foundation does not sell, rent, or share
your data with advertisers, data brokers, marketing platforms, or
any third party not listed above.

---

## 4. Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Active account data | Until you delete your account |
| Soft-deleted account data | 30 days after deletion → permanently removed |
| Audit logs (`broker_key_events`, login_attempts) | 2 years from creation |
| Postmark email send history | Per Postmark's policy (typically 45 days) |
| Backups | Up to 90 days, encrypted, rotated |

You can request expedited deletion of your account by emailing
`support@foundationbots.com`. We will process such requests within 30
days.

---

## 5. Security

- TLS 1.2+ on all dashboard + API traffic (Let's Encrypt cert via
  Traefik)
- Bcrypt / argon2id password hashing
- AES-256-GCM at-rest encryption for brokerage credentials
- Per-trade risk caps, drawdown halts, daily-loss circuit breaker,
  frozen-mode kill switch
- Email-OTP confirmation before first shadow→live flip
- Rate limiting on login, signup, broker-key validation, demo logins
- Server runs on a hardened Linux host with restricted access; the
  operator's `.env` file holding the master encryption key is mode 600

**Reporting a vulnerability:** Email `support@foundationbots.com` with
details. The operator will acknowledge within 72 hours and aim to
patch within 14 days for critical issues.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — visible in the dashboard at all times; raw
  export available on request
- **Correct** your data — update email + password from Settings
- **Delete** your data — account deletion flow + 30-day grace period
- **Export** your data — email `support@foundationbots.com` for a
  JSON dump

For users in jurisdictions with specific data-protection rights
(EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA, etc.), Foundation will honor verified
requests under those regimes. Foundation does not currently appoint
an EU/UK representative; if your jurisdiction's law requires one for
us to lawfully serve you, please email us before signing up.
